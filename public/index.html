How TimeLens works

  1. One photo and its location

    The site reads GPS metadata from your photo or requests your device location. You do not need to enter a place name.

  2. Finding historical evidence

    We identify the place from your photo and coordinates, research the three selected years and save the source links.

  3. Three reconstructions

    AI edits the original photo for each era, aiming to preserve the angle, light and weather. Perfect alignment and accuracy of every detail are not guaranteed.

Weather and light match your photo, rather than the actual weather on a historical date. Moving the timeline does not generate additional images.
